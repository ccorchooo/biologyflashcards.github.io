# Diagram image credits

The Graphs page includes 12 downloaded educational diagrams across 8 units. These images are reproduced without changing their content. They are local assets in the source edition and embedded as data URLs in the standalone HTML. Both editions work without an internet connection.

Each image retains the licence or public-domain status listed below. ## Reading the diagrams

The focus guides are concise revision prompts matched to the unit topics. Some source images include labels beyond core IGCSE content; the notes point out the main structures to learn. Numerical and lettered images have a visible English key. Blood colour conventions, anatomical left/right and the distinction between spinal cord and vertebral column are explained where relevant.

The white image background is intentional in both colour themes: transparent images include dark labels and must remain readable. Open a diagram to zoom from its fitted width to 400%, then scroll or swipe to pan. Source and licence links use the internet only when selected.
