## Reading the schematics

- Relative rates and relative hormone levels use arbitrary units. Their timing and shape support revision; the numbers are not universal biological constants.
- The osmotic zero crossing is an example estimate of equal water potential, not a fixed potato concentration.
- The microbial culture figure plots log10 of viable-cell count, with tick labels displaying powers of ten.
- The histogram uses equal-width height intervals and touching bars; the ABO chart uses separated categories. Both samples are invented.
- The model menstrual panels align at day 14 for illustration. Cycle timing varies.
- The respirometer axis explicitly measures displacement towards the organism chamber. Its slope is a speed proxy; multiply by capillary area to obtain volume per time.
- The energy pyramid is qualitative: its widths are not proportional to numerical energy data.
- The oxygen-sag figure refers to organic-waste pollution downstream, not direct oxygen consumption by fertiliser.
